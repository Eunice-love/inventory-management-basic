# Feature: Stock Adjustment & Cycle Counting

## Short Description
Allows staff to compare physical stock counts against system records
and correct differences, with a reason recorded for each change.

## Purpose
To keep system stock accurate despite damage, theft, loss, or
data-entry mistakes, and to maintain an audit trail.

## Main Functions/Activities
- Schedule cycle counts (daily/weekly/monthly, by category or location)
- Enter physical counts for each SKU
- Compare counted vs. system quantity and flag variances
- Record adjustment reasons (damaged, expired, lost, miscount)
- Require approval for large adjustments
- Log who adjusted, when, and why
- Generate a variance report
